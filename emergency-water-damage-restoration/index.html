When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in La Habra

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Learn the location of your breaker panel, which lets you safely kill power to a wet room.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
